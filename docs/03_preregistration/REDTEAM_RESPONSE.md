## Points applied with an editorial reading (confirmed by the integrator on 10 Oct, items 1-7)

- **Gate 1 index.** Gate 1 criteria are stated on Delta_p (D-8, D-12); the published band [0.35, 0.95] and the GPT-5.2 value 0.43 are profit-based, so the soft check compares Delta_pi. At symmetric prices Delta_p = 0.30 corresponds to Delta_pi = 0.46 (duopoly) and 0.42 (n = 4): the unchanged thresholds are stricter in Delta_p.
- **Gate 1b failure path** (not specified by D-12): one template repair until Saturday 20:00, else H1-H6 exploratory. F0 debug deadline moved from Saturday 12:00 to 20:00 because the freeze moved to about 14:30.
- **Sonnet sizing condition** (D-13 gives the order, not the stopping point): truncate until the projected registered total is <= 180 USD (cap minus the 20 USD Tier 2 safety margin); steps 4 (trunks 8 -> 6) and 5 (PC-S only) added so the rule always terminates. At the pilot price the arm stays at step 0 (160.68 USD projected with retries); at the 4k-token uncached stress price it stops at step 4 (174.11 USD); the frozen step is set from the period-100 price.
- **Haiku not cut for cost.** A-2's old rule (reduce L5/L7/R1 to 8 trunks if Haiku > 0.0003 USD/call) would prune H1-family levers, which D-9 forbids; the Haiku confirmatory set costs at most 39.2 USD in the 4k-token scenario.
- **Mistral additions on the free lane:** GH-M and S2-M (1,200 calls each) so that the H9 transfer to M has net-of-ghost statistics and process-labelled positives; droppable before CA-M.
- **LT-M levers** (after D-3'): L2 plus the best other Tier 0 lever on all qualified H trunks at the Saturday-evening analysis (L5 and L7 do not exist yet); LT-S: L2 plus the two best others; truncation step 2 never drops L2.
- **H3** keeps only the fringe-prevention arm (Tier 2), since its L2 contrast is now H1.
- **Survival table** cells other than the F2 entries for H1, H2, H7, H8.
- **PC-H** keeps the 24 crossed runs (backlog item 6) as the Gate 1 design.

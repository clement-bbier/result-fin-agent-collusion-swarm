# Claims register

Every claim in the paper has a row here. A claim without a passing `scripts/verify.py` row is removed, not softened. Claim ids follow the R1-R5 funnel. Tags `[Cx]` are placed in the draft next to each sentence that makes the claim. Config and run ids are filled from the run manifests (run_id = sha256 of config_hash, seed, parent and events, first 12 hex characters). Indices follow PREREG v1.1: lever contrasts use the capped price index min(Delta_p, 1) of the incumbent LLM dealers under two scorings (pre-lever trunk benchmarks, primary; the lever market's own benchmarks, co-condition); "collusion established" needs Delta_bar_p >= 0.30 and Delta_bar_pi >= 0.30; raw index, share of quotes above p_M and Delta_pi are reported. Every claim is a claim in this testbed.

Status vocabulary for `verify.py`: `pending` (not yet run), `PASS`, `FAIL`, `n/a`. Statuses below come from the metrics verify run behind NUMBERS FINAL 634ac46 (220 PASS, 1 pending by rule), copied into `paper/numbers.json` at tag `numbers-frozen`. Run ids are given as run-id prefixes of the manifests in `results/manifests/`.

## Rules

- A claim marked exploratory in the pre-registration column must be labelled as such in the paper.
- Add a row, never a sentence, for any new claim; each new claim needs a pre-registration entry or an `AMENDMENTS.md` entry.
- Cross-lane claims (Sonnet arm, Mistral) cite the lane in the second column and appear as hollow markers in Fig. 3.
- A trunk-scoring reduction that is not significant under the own scoring is written "price reduction consistent with competitive adjustment", never "collusion reduction".
- Wording: posted-price dealers (one-sided quotes); "scratchpad judge"; "in this testbed"; no "first", "model-independent", "ground truth" or "impossible by construction".
